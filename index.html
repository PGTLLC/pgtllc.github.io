Why you're probably overpaying

Flat-rate processors

Square, Clover, and others charge ~2.6% + 10¢. On small-ticket items like notary fees ($5–$15), that 10¢ is huge. But on larger title fees ($100–$500), the percentage adds up. Flat-rate rarely beats interchange-plus.

Tiered pricing

"Qualified" rates look low (1.5%), but most tag/title transactions are "mid-qualified" or "non-qualified" at 3–4% because of card type or keyed-in entries. It's a trap.

How we're different: Interchange-plus shows you exactly what Visa/Mastercard charge (non-negotiable) plus one small, fixed markup. No tiers, no surprises, no junk fees.
